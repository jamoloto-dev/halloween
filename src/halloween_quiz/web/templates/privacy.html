🎃 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Player Privacy First

Halloween Quiz is built with privacy by design. We believe trivia should be fun, spooky, and completely free of invasive data collection.

2. What We Collect

  • Display Names: The nickname you provide when playing is stored solely with your quiz score on the public leaderboard. We do not require or collect your real name.
  • Game Metrics: Scores, accuracy percentages, chosen difficulties, and timestamps are recorded strictly for high score leaderboards.
  • Local Preferences: Audio settings (volume, mute toggle) and achievements are stored directly on your device via localStorage. They never leave your browser.

3. What We Never Collect

We do not collect email addresses, personal identification, precise GPS locations, contact lists, or biometric data. We do not sell player data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Offline & PWA Storage

When installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), static assets (CSS, JS, sound effects, images) are cached locally on your device via the browser Cache API to provide instant loading.

5. Open Source & Transparency

Halloween Quiz is fully open-source. You can inspect the entire codebase and privacy implementation on GitHub at github.com/jamoloto-dev/halloween.
